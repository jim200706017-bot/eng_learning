# ☕ Charlie 口语练习记录

话题: OpenAI Corporate Structure & AI Regulation

阶段: Stage 2 (Guided)

## 对话摘要

**Charlie**: Hey JIMMY! Ready to chat about today's AI news? I saw Sam Altman testified in court yesterday. Let's discuss it — and try to use these words: **testify, equity, liability, precedent, governance**.

**Charlie**: So Altman had to **testify** about Musk's proposal. If you were in his shoes, how would you feel about someone wanting to pass company control to their children?

**JIMMY**: I think it's unreasonable. A company should have clear governance structure, not just pass to family.

**Charlie**: Great use of "governance"! Yeah, that's exactly the issue — who decides the **governance** of a company when it shifts from nonprofit to for-profit?

**Charlie**: Speaking of shift — the concept of **equity** is central here. OpenAI's founders had "sweat equity." In legal terms, what does **equity** mean to you as a law student?

**JIMMY**: Equity means fairness, but also a type of ownership in company law.

**Charlie**: Nice, you got both meanings! That's actually really relevant — there's a lot of discussion about who holds **equity** in AI companies.

**Charlie**: One more — **liability**. If an AI system makes a wrong decision, who bears the **liability**? This case could set a legal **precedent** for the whole industry.

## 🎯 本次口语练习反馈

### 亮点
- 使用 target word "governance" 很自然
- 对法律话题有深度，作为法学生背景恰当应用了专业词汇
- 能够区分 equity 的双重含义（公平 vs 股权）

### 改进点
- 可以尝试用完整句子回应而非短语（"Equity means fairness" → "Equity refers to fairness in legal contexts, but it can also mean ownership shares in a company"）
- 注意使用 "shift" 时搭配: shift **from** X **to** Y
